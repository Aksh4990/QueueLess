# QueueLess product brief

## Concept

QueueLess lets a student request food before reaching the canteen and select a pickup window. A canteen operator can review the incoming queue, prepare an order and mark it ready. The concept aims to make a short break more predictable while giving staff a simple view of upcoming pickup requests.

## Problem hypothesis

During a short campus break, waiting to order and collect food may take time students would rather use to eat, rest or travel between classes. A small advance-order flow might make collection more predictable. This is a hypothesis to test, not a verified finding.

## Primary users

**Students** need a quick way to understand the menu, choose a pickup window and know when food is ready.

**Canteen operators** need to understand the requested items and pickup timing without adding a complex workflow during busy periods.

## Core journey

1. Student browses the menu and adds items to a basket.
2. Student chooses a pickup window and previews the request.
3. The request appears in a sample canteen queue.
4. The operator moves the request through preparation and ready states.
5. Student sees the updated status and collects the order.

## MVP scope shown here

- Sample menu search and category filters.
- Basket quantities, sample total and pickup window.
- Student order list with status timeline.
- Canteen queue view with manual status changes.
- Simple menu availability controls.

## Deliberately out of scope

Live inventory, online payment, accounts, notifications, refunds, analytics, and integration with institute or canteen systems. These require workflow research, operational agreement and appropriate technical safeguards before implementation.

## Value to investigate

- For students: whether advance selection and pickup status reduce uncertainty during breaks.
- For operators: whether a timed request queue is usable alongside walk-up service.
- For both: whether the pickup promise can be met when demand changes.
